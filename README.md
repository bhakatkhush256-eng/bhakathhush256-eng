### 💼 Featured Projects

#### [VelvetVault Commerce Insights](https://github.com/bhakatkhush256-eng/ecommerce-sales-customer-insights-sql)
E-commerce analysis built on a custom-designed database (`luxe_market_db`). Explored customer purchasing patterns and revenue drivers through SQL, with findings framed as concrete business recommendations.
`SQL` `Customer Analytics` `Database Design`

#### [Healthcare Operations Analytics](https://github.com/bhakatkhush256-eng/hospital-performance-analysis-sql)
Analyzed hospital admission and department-level data to surface operational patterns — treatment cost variance, admission timing, and resource-planning signals — using SQL.
`SQL` `Healthcare Analytics` `KPI Analysis`

#### [Workforce Intelligence (HR Analytics)](https://github.com/bhakatkhush256-eng/hr-analytics-sql)
SQL-based analysis of employee data exploring compensation, tenure, and department-level retention patterns to identify attrition risk factors.
`SQL` `HR Analytics` `Workforce Planning`

#### [Multi-City Food Delivery Analysis](https://github.com/bhakatkhush256-eng/food-delivery-analysis)
Compared delivery performance metrics across multiple Pakistani cities to identify operational and demand pattern differences.
`SQL` `Market Analysis` `Operational Analytics`

#### [PSL Cricket Performance Analysis](https://github.com/bhakatkhush256-eng/SQL-analysis-of-PSL-team-performance-2023-2024)
Statistical analysis of the 2023–24 Pakistan Super League season — player performance metrics, match-level trends, and team scorecards extracted from semi-structured sports data.
`SQL` `Sports Analytics` `Statistical Modeling`
